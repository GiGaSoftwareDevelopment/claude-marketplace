# Setup

Two audience-specific setup guides — pick the one that matches your role.

## I'm an end user, stakeholder, or note-taker

You'll be **creating** notes — talking through your work with Claude and capturing summaries, decisions, and attached media for the team. You'll use **Claude Cowork** (the desktop chat app).

→ **[SETUP-END-USER.md](SETUP-END-USER.md)** — non-developer walkthrough with screenshots; can be opened directly inside a Cowork session and guided through interactively.

## I'm a software engineer

You'll be **consuming** the notes — reading captures, searching the corpus, building tooling, contributing during feature work, or maintaining the plugin. You'll use **Claude Code** (the CLI).

→ **[SETUP-DEV-TEAM.md](SETUP-DEV-TEAM.md)** — terse, engineer-paced; assumes a developer-machine baseline.
